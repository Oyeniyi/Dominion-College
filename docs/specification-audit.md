# PRD Design-Readiness Audit

**Audit date:** 2026-10-27  
**Document audited:** [`../prd.md`](../prd.md), draft version 1.0  
**Related project guidance:** [`../AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md)

## Executive Summary

The PRD provides a useful product vision and broad MVP outline for a public school website, a protected content-management dashboard, and admissions enquiries. It identifies important safety principles: only publish approved school information, minimize personal data, protect administrator-only records, and avoid treating the enquiry MVP as a full admission application. It also names user journeys, major features, and a launch checklist.

It is **not yet design-ready for a complete implementation or launch commitment**. Critical operating decisions remain open, particularly data governance for enquiries and images of minors, administrator lifecycle and permissions, retention/deletion, and who approves and follows up on public content and enquiries. Multiple requirements use qualitative terms without measurable acceptance conditions. The document proposes a stack but does not select one or record hosting, budget, volume, availability, or operational constraints. The repository currently contains only `prd.md` and `AGENTS.md`; there is no code, asset set, manifest, test suite, or implementation documentation to resolve these uncertainties.

No product changes are made by this audit. Findings below are **open audit issues**, not approved changes to scope. A safe next step is requirements clarification and approval of the high-severity decisions before committing to data, security, architecture, and release designs. Discovery, approved content inventory, and low-risk design exploration can proceed in parallel.

## Scope and Method

- Reviewed the full draft PRD and repository-level `AGENTS.md`.
- Inspected the workspace structure for relevant specifications, code, tests, manifests, scripts, and assets. Only `prd.md` and `AGENTS.md` were present; no product implementation or other referenced documentation was available.
- Audited problem definition, users and journeys, functional/non-functional requirements, business rules, data, security/privacy, integrations, failure behavior, accessibility, performance/scalability, AI-related scope, acceptance criteria, metrics, deployment, constraints, and dependencies.
- Referenced PRD sections and requirement IDs rather than page numbers because the Markdown document has no stable pagination. Section 5 (Functional Requirements) was reviewed requirement by requirement.
- Severity reflects the potential design/rework/safety impact, not a finding that a defect exists in unbuilt software.
- Finding statuses are **Open**: no decision or PRD amendment has been approved as part of this audit.

## Unresolved Business Questions

The following decisions require a human owner. Proposed answers must not be treated as approved facts.

1. **Business outcome:** What baseline period and target should define a useful admissions enquiry result, and who owns measurement and follow-up?
2. **Content authority:** Who may draft, review, approve, and publish institutional copy, admissions details, news, and student photographs? Is a second-person approval required for any class?
3. **Administrator access:** Who owns the initial administrator account, account recovery, access review, and staff departure/deactivation process? Which MVP roles/actions are required?
4. **Enquiry operations:** Who responds, within what operational expectation, what statuses/transitions are used, and what happens to uncontacted or duplicate enquiries?
5. **Privacy governance:** What retention period, privacy notice, consent/acknowledgement wording, access/export process, and deletion/request procedure are approved? Who handles suspected exposure?
6. **Student media:** What authorization is required before publication, how is it recorded, and how are removal requests and already distributed/cached copies handled?
7. **Managed content:** Which exact About, Academics, Admissions, contact, footer, and settings fields need editing in the MVP? Which require approval before publication?
8. **Operational constraints:** What budget, hosting/domain ownership, preferred vendors, data-location constraints, expected traffic/media growth, support capacity, and recovery expectations apply?
9. **Integrations:** Is email notification or analytics required for launch? Which approved WhatsApp number, email, social accounts, map/address, and analytics approach may be used?
10. **Accessibility and launch gate:** Who accepts accessibility evidence and exceptions, and which requirements are release-blocking?
11. **AI-assisted work:** May AI tools process school content or media, and under what data classification and approval rules? Current PRD scope excludes AI runtime features.

## Recommended PRD Amendments

These are **proposals for owner review**, not approved scope changes:

1. Add a decision/approval register with owner, status, date, and affected requirements for open decisions in Sections 9, 12, and 20.
2. Add measurable acceptance criteria for enquiry submission/retry behavior, content publication freshness, accessibility evidence, performance checks, and launch-blocking tests.
3. Add a compact role/action matrix and content-approval workflow, including image authorization and removal.
4. Add an enquiry and media data-handling schedule: purpose, access, retention, deletion, user requests, audit, and incident escalation.
5. Add an integration/operations register documenting providers, data exchanged, owners, failure handling, cost/constraints, and approval state.
6. Define editable content, data lifecycle/status transitions, metric semantics, URL/timezone policy, and dashboard metric labels.
7. Record expected usage and deployment/recovery assumptions, then validate them with the selected hosting and operational owners.
8. Explicitly confirm that AI runtime capabilities remain excluded from MVP and define permitted AI-tool use with non-public data.
9. Maintain traceability from each requirement and launch criterion to its owner and verification evidence. Do not invent task IDs; establish a task-ID scheme only if the project adopts one.

## Design-Readiness Assessment

**Assessment: Conditional / not ready for full implementation baseline or production launch approval.**

| Area | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Product vision and broad scope | **Partially ready.** Goals, MVP page list, principal users, and explicit non-goals are useful. The problem impact and success targets need validation. |
| UX and functional design | **Partially ready.** Core public/admin capabilities are identified, but content fields, roles, approval steps, status rules, and error/recovery behavior need decisions. |
| Data and privacy | **Not ready for schema sign-off.** Candidate entities are useful, but retention, access, deletion, media consent evidence, audit, and operational privacy rules remain unresolved. |
| Security | **Directionally strong, not design-complete.** Important controls are named, but administrator lifecycle, permission matrix, abuse behavior, incident operations, and deployment boundaries are open. |
| Accessibility and performance | **Targets identified, verification incomplete.** WCAG and CWV references are present, but test conditions, evidence, scale assumptions, and service expectations need definition. |
| Integrations and deployment | **Not ready for vendor/production decisions.** Hosting, domain, storage, authentication, notification, analytics, budget, and ownership are unconfirmed. |
| Acceptance and traceability | **Partially ready.** Launch checklist exists, but several criteria are subjective and do not consistently map to evidence or owners. |
| AI-specific behavior | **Acceptable for current scope with clarification.** Runtime AI is a non-goal; AI-tool handling of non-public information should be explicitly governed. |

**Can proceed now:** requirements clarification, verified content and asset inventory, low-risk information architecture/design exploration, and drafting the missing approval/operations decisions.  
**Gate before committing to full implementation design:** resolve or explicitly defer the high-severity findings AUD-03 through AUD-08, approve architecture constraints (AUD-16), and identify deployment/data owners (AUD-17).  
**Gate before launch:** approve privacy and media policies, verified contact/admissions content, access/retention operations, accessibility and security verification, and deployment/recovery evidence.

## Verification and Limitations

- Reviewed `prd.md` and `AGENTS.md`; inspected the full workspace file listing.
- No source code, tests, dependency manifests, development scripts, design documents, architecture documents, data schema, security specification, or deployment documentation were present to corroborate planned behavior.
- No tests were run: this deliverable is a requirements audit document, not a product-code change.
- This audit identifies specification readiness risks; it is not legal advice, a penetration test, an accessibility conformance audit, or validation of school facts.
